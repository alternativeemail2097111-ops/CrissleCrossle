❓ How to Play CrissleCrossle LIVE

The goal

The host starts a round with a secret 5-letter word. Viewers guess by typing a 5-letter word by itself in the LIVE chat (e.g. just APPLE, not "I think it's apple!"). Every valid word guessed gets tested and added to the board. First person to type the exact secret word wins the round.

The twist: a decoy word every guess

Here's what makes it tricky - after every guess, a fresh blue decoy word is also chosen. A tile turns green if the letter matches that position in the real answer OR the decoy. A tile turns yellow if the letter is somewhere in the answer or the decoy, just not that exact spot. So a green tile is a good sign, but it is not proof - it might only be green because of the decoy!

Example: guessing CLAIM while the decoy that round was CRIME
vs. decoy
  • C is green - matches the decoy's C in spot 1, so this alone doesn't confirm the real answer starts with C.
  • L is yellow - the decoy has no L at all, so this yellow can only be coming from the real answer (just not in spot 2).
  • A is grey - not in the answer or the decoy anywhere.
  • I is yellow - the decoy has an I (in a different spot), so this one's ambiguous.
  • M is green - the decoy's M is in a different spot, so this green must be from the real answer.

Guessing & hints

  • Unlimited guesses - there's no attempt limit, only a round timer.
  • Only guesses that are real 5-letter words get tested and shown on the board - random letters are ignored.
  • The host can drop a free hint letter any time, and one also appears automatically every few guesses.
  • If nobody solves it before time runs out, the answer is revealed and a new round begins.

Scoring

  • +1 point just for making your first valid guess each round.
  • Solving it earns a bonus - bigger the fewer guesses it took, bigger still if you didn't need any hints, plus an extra bonus for solving within the round's first third.
  • Top 10 all-time scores are shown on the leaderboard.
